How it works

The menu, already there

Add a restaurant and its menu appears: shared by other members, read from the restaurant's own website, or turned into a checklist from a photo you snap.

Rate it. Note it. Remember it.

Give each dish stars and a note for next time. Your notes stay, even when a dish leaves the menu.

Quests, badges, levels

Every menu comes with quests: try every appetizer, or clear the whole menu. Earn badges, level up, and get a little confetti.

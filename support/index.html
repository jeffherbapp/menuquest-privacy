Getting MenuQuest

How do I get the app?

MenuQuest is in private beta for iPhone. Request an invite, and when it arrives, install Apple's free TestFlight app and open the invite link on your iPhone.

Is there an Android or web version?

Not yet. MenuQuest is iPhone-only for now.

Menus

A restaurant's menu didn't appear. What can I do?

Tap the camera on the restaurant's page and photograph the menu. MenuQuest turns it into a checklist, and the next person to add that restaurant gets it too. Got more pages? Scan them as well and they're added to the same menu.

The menu is wrong or out of date.

On the restaurant's page, tap ⋯ and choose “Report a problem with this menu.” You can also rescan it: dishes that have left the menu are kept with your notes, marked as no longer available.

Your account and data

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. Signing in (with Apple or an email link) is optional, and will let your ratings count toward community recommendations when they arrive.

Where are my ratings and notes stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own iCloud if it's turned on, so they come back if you reinstall or switch phones. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings › Account › Delete account. Your restaurants, ratings and notes stay on your phone.

Restaurants

I run a restaurant. How does MenuQuest get my menu?

From diners who scan it, and from your own website. See MenuQuest for restaurants for how it works and how to opt out or get a menu corrected.
